Limits of the service

The tools are provided free of charge and without warranty, in line with the licence. Every tool page states precisely what the conversion keeps and what it loses; those limits are real and documented, and worth reading before you process an important document.

Two points bear repeating here. Editing text covers the old content without removing it from the file: that is not redaction. The handwritten signature offered is the digital equivalent of initialling a page, not a qualified electronic signature under the eIDAS regulation.

Always keep a copy of your original documents.

Personal data

Data processing is described on the Privacy page. In short: no cookies, no analytics, and your documents do not leave your computer.
