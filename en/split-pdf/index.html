    What the conversion keeps, and what it loses

    Working inside a browser comes with limits a hosted service does not have. Here they are in plain words, so you know what to expect before you start.

    What is kept

    • The exact quality of the pages
    • Selectable text and embedded fonts
    • Page sizes, even when they differ
    • The original order inside each piece

    What is not carried over

    • Bookmarks and the table of contents
    • Filled form fields
    • Electronic signatures, which become void

    Need the other direction? Merge PDFs does exactly the opposite, with the same guarantees.

    Worth knowing about the format

    From two pieces onwards the files are gathered into a ZIP archive your system opens with a double click. Each file carries its page or range number.

    Why it's free

    Online converters receive your file on their servers, process it, then promise to delete it. Every document costs them computing time: that is why they charge, and why they cap file sizes.

    Here, the work happens on your own computer. The page contains no server code: your document cannot be received, because it is never sent. So there is nothing to bill, nothing to cap, and nothing to delete afterwards. Cut your connection once the page has loaded — everything keeps working.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I write the ranges?

    Separate them with commas: “1-3, 4, 5-12” produces three files. A single number gives a single page. Ranges may overlap without trouble.

    Does splitting reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied identically, with no re-encoding: text stays text and images keep their resolution.

    What if I just want to cut it in two?

    Pick the range mode and type for instance “1-10, 11-20”: you get two files.
