    Worth knowing about the format

    This is a handwritten signature placed visually, like initials on paper. It is not a qualified electronic signature: that requires a certificate issued by a trusted provider and a dedicated infrastructure.

    Why it's free

    Online converters receive your file on their servers, process it, then promise to delete it. Every document costs them computing time: that is why they charge, and why they cap file sizes.

    Here, the work happens on your own computer. The page contains no server code: your document cannot be received, because it is never sent. So there is nothing to bill, nothing to cap, and nothing to delete afterwards. Cut your connection once the page has loaded — everything keeps working.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does this signature have legal value?

    It is the digital equivalent of handwritten initials: widely accepted in everyday commercial exchanges, on the same footing as a scanned contract. A qualified electronic signature under eIDAS is a different thing and requires a certified provider.

    Is my signature stored anywhere?

    No. It is drawn in the tab, placed on the document, then gone when you close the page. Nothing is transmitted or kept.

    Can I sign somewhere other than the last page?

    Yes: give the page number you want, and set the position with the three lists provided.
